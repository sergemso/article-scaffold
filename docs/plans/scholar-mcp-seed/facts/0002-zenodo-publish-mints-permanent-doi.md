## Fact

Once a Zenodo deposit is published its files and DOI cannot be deleted; only a new version can be added. A draft, and the Zenodo sandbox, have no such effect.

## Consequence

Publish is the one irreversible action in the authors server, hence the out-of-band confirmation and the sandbox default (decision 0005).
